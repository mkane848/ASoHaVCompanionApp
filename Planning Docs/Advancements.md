### XP, The Potential Track, and Character Advancement

**Party Advancement | Rapport**  
To gain an advancement on your party’s Playbook, you need to mark **Rapport** five times. When you mark your fifth, clear your **Rapport Track** and choose one unique option from your Party Playbook’s advancement list. 

You are restricted to the Tier 1 options at first; however, once you have taken four advancements from Tier 1 you unlock the Tier 2 options. Once you have taken 3 more advancements or either Tier 1 or Tier 2, you may now also choose from the Tier 3 options. Once you have taken a total of 10 Advancements, you may now take Tier 4 options. 

\[1 Rapport\] Short Term Boon  
\[5 Rapport\] Take a Party Playbook Advancement

**Help \- Short Term Boon**

Anyone in the Party can spend Rapport by removing it from the Rapport Track to give any other party member \+1 on a move, even after they roll. In order to spend Rapport this way, the spender needs to truly be able to offer aid. Describe how you do so in fiction before giving the \+1. 

Any and all party members can spend **Rapport** from the Track to help on that same roll. Each teammate can only spend one Rapport themselves. Using Rapport this way can take a total miss to a complete success\! 

Add one **Rapport** when you **Enter Combat**.

* If everyone has the same end goal for the fight, add another Rapport.  
* If your party is ill-prepared or off-balance, remove a Rapport.

You mark **Rapport** at the end of each session when you take the **End the Session** move.  

**Playbook Advancement | Potential**  
Certain moves and missing any roll will have you mark **Potential**. To take an advancement on your character playbook, you need to mark potential five times. When you mark your fifth potential, clear your Experience Track and choose one unique option from your Playbook’s advancement list. 

You are restricted to the options above the line at first; however, once you have taken five advancements from that top section, you unlock the ability to choose from the more advanced options listed below the line.

**Social Advancement | Kin**

* Bond Track: Found on the Party Playbook. A Progress track from 0 \- 5 that shows how well PCs know and feel about one another. Each PC has a Bond Track with each other PC.   
    
* Kin: Kin is to the Bond Track as XP is to the Experience Track.   
  * Kin is gained on a per Bond Track basis.   
  * Kin is gained for various moves and results in the game.   
  * Either PC on the Bond Track can spend Kin to enact various, immediate effects.   
  * At 5 Kin, those PCs can Forge a Bond. When you Forge a Bond, both PCs on the track agree to select a Bond Move from the list of Bond Moves available to your Bond Level.

**Forging Bonds**  
After you and another PC Forge a Bond, your Bond Level goes from 0 to 1\. After 5 more Kin, you advance from 1 to 2 and so on up to Bond Level 5\. 

If you spend a Kin and it would take you below 0, drop your Bond Level down 1 with four Kin marked. 

When you place your 5th Kin at Bond 5, your Bond Level locks and can not be moved down. You can no longer spend Kin on that track and you gain a SUPER COOL Bond move. 

At 0 Bond, all characters have a SHOT IN THE DARK (define that) move with each other. High risk. 

Spending Kin  
Spend a Kin to influence a roll involving that fellow party member. You may choose to do this after the roll is made:

* \+1 to your roll against them or an action you take that they see and oppose.  
* \-1 to their roll against you or an action they take that you see and oppose.   
* Offer them an experience point to do what you want.  
* Add an extra harm (1 for 1\) to whatever harm you’re dealing them.  
* Mark a Condition on them or give them a Rank 2 Status. 

Remember: to do it, do it. You can't just spend the point; you have to describe what your character actually does to earn the bonus. If the math changes, something in the story has to change first to cause it.
