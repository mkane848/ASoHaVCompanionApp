## You like it, you love it, you need it

### **Virtues**

#### How your character chooses to face the world

| *The Virtue* | *Its Essense* | *Use it when you want…* |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Might** | **Physicality & Force**. Your raw power, athleticism, and physical capabilities. | To **smash** through a gate, **wrestle** a beast, or **climb** a mountain.  |
| **Mettle** | **Willpower & Grit**. Your internal fire, your bravery in the face of the unknown, and your spiritual resilience | To **stare down** a dragon’s gaze, **resist the temptation** of a cursed relic, or **keep moving** when your legs want to give out.   |
| **Heart** | **Bond & Empathy.** Your ability to connect with others, to inspire hope, and to act on your deepest convictions. | To **convince** a village to stand and fight, **comfort** a grieving companion, or **sacrifice your own safety** for the group's sake. |
| **Wit** | **Perception & Instinct**. Your sharpness of mind, your ability to read a room, and your eye for the hidden or the mysterious. | To **find** the hidden latch in a ruin, **realize** an NPC is hiding a secret, or **notice** the ambush before the arrows fly. |
| **Guile** | **Strategy & Subtlety**. Your cleverness, your dexterity, and your ability to outmaneuver or outthink an opponent. | To **sneak** past a sentry, **pick** a complex lock, set a clever **trap**, or **misdirect** a foe’s attention during a tense parley. |

### **Conditions**

#### Negative emotional states affecting your character.

Having a **Virtue** marked with a **Condition** gives you *\-2 Ongoing* on all rolls (with a floor of \-3 total) with that **Virtue** until the **Condition** is cleared. 

You can **clear a Condition** through \[some BASIC MOVES???\], or by taking the **Clear Action** detailed below.  

| *The Virtue* | *The Condition* | *Clear Action* |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Might** | **Exhausted** | Recover from your exhaustion by indulging in a vice and shirking responsibility. |
| **Mettle** | **Afraid** | Come down from your fear by running away from something challenging.  |
| **Heart** | **Hopeless** | Absolve your guilt by punishing yourself or blame a party member related to your guilt. |
| **Wit** | **Irrational** | Vent your anger by hurting someone important to you or damage something important.  |
| **Guile** | **Distracted** | Get over your insecurity by making a rash action without discussing it with the party. |

When you would mark a **Condition** but have no **Virtues** left to mark, your character is **Dishonored** (introduce a clock to yourself? Or a Hard Move like consequences? How does its all click?). Something gets between you and your quest  
	\> why is it taking them away, and why now?

### **Statuses**

#### Personal, flexible **clocks**.

**Statuses** have a **Rank** that specifies their intensity from *1 (mild)* to *6 (deadly or transformative)*.
