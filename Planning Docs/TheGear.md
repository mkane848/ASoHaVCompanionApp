## Load & Item Charges

**Load**  
On your Journey, you have access to all of the items on your character sheet. After you **Make Camp**, decide what your character’s Load will be. While on your Journey you can declare, at any time, that your character has any item they have access to on their person right now by checking the box for that item \- up to a number of items equal to your chosen Load. Your Load also determines your speed and conspicuousness. 

* 3 Load is Light. You move quickly, have \+1 Speed in Combat, and are relatively inconspicuous.   
* 5 Load is Normal. You move normally and look like a prepared adventurer.   
* 6 Load is Heavy. You move slower than normal, have \-1 Speed in Combat, and look like a particularly armed combatant. 

Your character’s Light, Normal, and Heavy Loads are modified by your Might Virtue. A Might \+1 means that 4 is your Light Load, 6 is your Normal, and 7 is your Heavy. Similarly if your Might is \-1 then 2 is your Light, 4 is your Normal, and 5 is your Heavy. 

Your Load represents how obvious and bulky your gear is to anyone looking at you. Small, easily concealed items like a hidden boot knife cost 0-Load and don't count against your limit. Most standard equipment, such as your sword, shield, or armor, are clearly visible and cost 1-Load each. Particularly large or cumbersome items might cost 2-Load. 

Once you check your last Load box, you can not use any new items until you **Make Camp**. 

**Charges**  
Some items have Charges. You can use that item until you mark each charge, then you must find a way to refresh it (often at a Merchant in exchange for Wealth).
