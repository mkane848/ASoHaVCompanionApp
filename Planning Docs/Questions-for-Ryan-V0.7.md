# Questions for Ryan — Ruleset V0.7

Prepared 2026-10-24 for Ryan McGilloway, co-designer of *A Story of Heroes and Villains*.

## What this is

The companion app now follows Ruleset V0.7. Wherever V0.7 contradicts itself, stays silent, or marks
something with a "???", we did **not** guess. We picked the most cautious behaviour (usually "show a
reminder and let the table decide") and wrote the question down. This document gathers all 36 of those
questions in one place so you can settle them in a single sitting.

Nothing here is urgent and nothing is broken. Every item works today in the cautious way described. Your
answers just tell us whether to keep it, change it, or build something more.

## How to answer

- Reply **by number**. "Option B" is enough. Example: "7: B. 12: A. 20: your call."
- If none of the options fit, write your own answer in the space under "Ryan's answer:".
- "Not decided yet" is a fine answer. We will keep the cautious default and ask again later.
- Line numbers in brackets refer to the V0.7 document, so you can find the passage fast.
- Allow about 30 minutes. Sections are independent, so you can answer them in any order.

## Combat

### 1. Legendary turns after a Team-Up

**The question.** When two Heroes take a Team-Up, how many turns does a Legendary Enemy get?

**What V0.7 says.** Team-Up Turns: "A Legendary Enemy will take two turns in a row if the Heroes take a
Team-Up Turn" (line 5821). Legendary: "takes one turn after every Hero's turn" (line 6030). A Team-Up is two
Hero turns, so those sentences can give two turns in a row or one after each Hero.

**What the app does today.** It keeps its older behaviour (one turn after each Hero's turn) and does not add the "two in a row" rule.

**Options.**
- A. Two turns in a row after the pair (line 5821). The Legendary hits harder in response to a Team-Up.
- B. One turn after each Hero, so the Legendary acts between the two Team-Up partners (line 6030). Team-Ups give the Legendary no extra pressure.
- C. Something else (write it in).

### 2. Can Heroes answer a GM-started Team-Up

**The question.** The GM may now start a Team-Up for enemies. Can the Heroes respond with a Team-Up of their own, and does that cost anything?

**What V0.7 says.** Team-Up Turns describe only Heroes starting one (lines 5820–5821). The GM side is a ruling we made, not text from V0.7.

**What the app does today.** A GM Team-Up costs the GM 1 Drama when it is set up, a Hero Team-Up is free, and "Heroes may answer" is only a note, with no cost and no timing limit.

**Options.**
- A. Heroes may answer freely, at any point after the enemy pair. The simplest table experience.
- B. Heroes may answer, but only immediately after the enemy pair acts. Keeps the exchange tight.
- C. Heroes may answer, and it costs something (name it). Keeps it a real trade-off.
- D. Heroes may not answer.

**Ryan's answer:**


### 3. Potential when Combat ends

**The question.** Does each Hero mark Potential every time Combat ends, or only when the Heroes achieve the Combat Goal?

**What V0.7 says.** "when Combat ends, mark 1 Potential on one Motif whose Skill or Flaw Tag was used" (line 5854) reads as always. "When Combat ends in achieving the Goal: … Each Hero marks Potential for one Motif used during Combat" (lines 5971 and 5974) reads as success only.

**What the app does today.** Potential is awarded on success only.

**Options.**
- A. Success only (line 5974). Losing a fight earns nothing.
- B. Always, win or lose (line 5854). Defeat still teaches the Heroes something.
- C. Always, but a failed Goal marks it only on a Motif whose Flaw Tag was used (or another split you prefer).

### 4. When Action Points really refill

**The question.** Do Action Points refill at the end of the Hero's own turn, or only at the end of their next turn?

**What V0.7 says.** "At the *end* of the Hero's turn, restore their AP to their maximum" (line 5828), but the next paragraph says AP "won't reset until that next turn ends" (line 5830). The second Combat section says "AP spent between the Hero's turn is restored at the end of their next turn" (line 5860).

**What the app does today.** AP refills to the maximum at the end of the Hero's own turn, and anything spent between turns comes back at the end of their next turn.

**Options.**
- A. Keep as is: refill at the end of each of the Hero's own turns. Heroes begin every turn with full AP.
- B. Refill happens only at the end of the *next* turn. A Hero who spends AP on reactions starts their next turn short, which makes reactions a real cost.
- C. Something else (write it in).

**Options.**
- A. One box absorbs the whole hit. Temporary Strain is a strong buffer.

### 9. What "one source" means for Armor

**What the app does today.** Armor can be marked against any single hit, Heavy needs the GM to say the hit lands on the body, and Special needs the GM's tag to match a listed effect; with no GM tag, the table decides.

**Options.**
- A. One source = one attack or effect, however many Strain boxes it would mark. Multi-hit attacks need one Armor box per hit.
- B. One source = one enemy per turn, so a flurry from the same enemy is covered by one box.
- C. Leave it to the table (the app only reminds).
- Also tell us how Special Armor effects should be worded (a short list of keywords, or free text).

### 20. What a "second mark" means
