# Audit and code review, 2026-11-12

A full read of the server, the migrations, the shared normalizers and the web client's data layer,
plus a measured UI/UX pass across phones, tablets and PC monitors. The app was at `0.82.0`.
`0.83.0` fixes the findings marked **Fixed**; the rest are open and summarized in `HANDOFF.md`
open issue 26.

## How it was done, and what was not checked

- **Code:** every file under `apps/server/src` (all routes, `repo.ts`), all 17 migrations,
  `packages/shared/src/logic.ts`'s normalizers, and `apps/web/src/lib` (API client, mutations,
  Realtime). The web feature panels were sampled, not read end to end.
- **Tests:** the suite ran before and after (`npm test`, `npm run typecheck`, `npm run lint`).
  Finding 1's new test was also run against the old `repo.ts` and failed there, which confirms the
  defect.
- **UI:** the responsive smoke test and the screenshot script, in Chromium, against the harness
  fixtures, at the existing ten viewports plus eight new ones (320×568, 844×390, 744×1133,
  1133×744, 1210×834, 1366×1024 touch, 1366×768, 1536×864).
- **Not checked:** the live site and database (the Supabase project is paused, finding 0, and the
  sandbox cannot reach either host); iOS-only behaviour (on-screen keyboard, toolbar, zoom on
  focus, standalone mode); WebKit; Bond row locking against real Postgres; whether finding 2's
  accounts exist in production.

## Correctness and security

**1. Every character-sheet read wrote the sheet back. Fixed.** `normalizeSheet` builds `Statuses`
with `.filter()`, which always returns a new array, and `repo.ts` decided whether to heal by
comparing sixteen fields by reference. The answer was always yes. So `getSheet` and
`listSheetsForCampaign` issued a write per sheet on every read; each write emitted a Realtime
event; `liveSync.ts` refetches on every event, so an open campaign page read and wrote in a loop;
and a read that raced a player's save could write the older copy back over it. Introduced around
2026-09-23. All 18 route test files mock `repo.ts`, so nothing exercised it. The heal check now
compares serialized content, `getParty` does the same (it compared 2 of about 20 fields), and
`repo.sheet.test.ts` covers the read path.

**2. Demo accounts with a published password were seeded into any empty database. Fixed going
forward; production still needs checking.** `seed.ts` ran regardless of `NODE_ENV` and creates
eight `@asohav.dev` users with password `asohav-dev`, five of them content admins; `README.md`
publishes the password. A content admin can generate a recovery link for any account. Demo
accounts are now skipped when `NODE_ENV=production` unless `SEED_DEMO_DATA=true`. **Open, owner
action:** once the database is back, check for `@asohav.dev` users and delete them or change their
passwords.

*Since the audit:* production had five such accounts, all still on the published password, two of
them content admins and one the GM of every campaign. On 2026-11-14, at the owner's direction,
each was given a random password and its sessions were removed. The accounts were kept.

**3. Three cross-campaign authorization gaps. Fixed.** Anyone can create a campaign and be its
GM, so "GM of the campaign in the URL" proves nothing about a record from another campaign.

| Route | Gap | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /campaigns/:c/sheets/:characterId` | A GM of any campaign could read any sheet by id. | The character must belong to the campaign. |
| `DELETE /campaigns/:c/invites/:inviteId` | Deleted any invite by id. | The invite must belong to the campaign, as resend already required. |
| `POST /campaigns/:c/combat/:id/end` | `Participants[].RefId` comes from the member-writable encounter PUT; ending Combat cleared that sheet's Strain and re-saved it under the caller's campaign, which also changed who passes the sheet's RLS policy. | Only this campaign's characters are read and saved. |

Each needed a known id. Ids were 8 base-36 characters of `Math.random()`; `newId` now draws 16
from the platform CSPRNG, and invite codes use `crypto.randomInt`. Existing ids are unchanged.

**4. Invite emails put the campaign name into HTML unescaped. Fixed.** Any user could name a
campaign with markup and mail it to an existing account from the app's own domain. The name, link
and code are escaped; the address is shape-checked; a campaign is capped at 20 pending invites.
**Open:** there is still no rate limit on any route.

**5. `listAuthUsers()` read only the first page of users. Fixed.** Supabase's default page is 50,
not the 1,000 the comment claimed. Past 50 accounts the admin Users view was truncated and
`sendInviteEmail` treated an existing account as new. It now pages through every user.

**6. `ilike` was used with unescaped input. Fixed.** `_` and `%` are wildcards. An account whose
email contains `_` was shown pending invites addressed to other matching emails (name and code;
redeeming was still blocked), and an invite code of `%` returned a 500.

**7. Whole-document saves have no concurrency check. Open.** Encounter, Party, Clock, World and
Sheet are last-write-wins. The docs call Bonds the one place with real concurrency risk, but an
encounter is written by every player and the GM during a fight, and the sheet now has five
server-side writers besides its owner. The library's `updated_at` precondition in `saveLibrary`
is the pattern to reuse: send the version read, refuse on mismatch, refetch and reapply.

**8. Whole-document PUT bodies are stored unvalidated. Open.** The sheet PUT stores `req.body`
without `normalizeSheet`; party, encounter, clock and world spread arbitrary keys into the row.
One malformed save reaches every client through bootstrap and Realtime.

**9. A library record update could change the record's `Id`. Fixed.** The PUT spread the body
last; the create route already guarded this. **Open:** library import writes unconditionally after
checking two keys.

**10. Multi-step writes are not atomic. Open.** Campaign creation (4 writes), character creation
(5 writes; a double submit can create two characters) and invite redemption can stop half-done.

**11. Hardening. Open.** No security headers; `pg` runs with `rejectUnauthorized: false`; the
`profiles` policy lets any signed-in user list every name and `is_admin` flag; the admin
password-reset action leaves no audit entry; a Supabase outage during token verification is
reported as 401, so clients look signed out.

**Smaller, open:** `/auth/me` and two admin routes load every profile per request; clock,
encounter and adventure PUTs fetch all rows to find one; `getSheet` normalizes with a Strain
length of 5 rather than the library setting; `normalizeSheet` blanks all Motifs if the array is
not exactly 3 long.

## UI/UX across devices

What was already sound: 44px touch targets enforced in CI, 16px inputs, hover gated behind
`(hover: hover)`, `dvh` on dialogs, contained scroll chaining, reduced motion, container queries
inside panels. Items already in `HANDOFF.md` open issue 25 are not repeated.

Measured result: all eight new viewports pass the smoke test's checks (no horizontal overflow, no
page errors, 44×44 targets, no overlapping hit areas) **except 320px**.

**U1. iPad mini portrait got the phone layout. Fixed.** The sheet went two-column at 768px; an
iPad mini is 744px. The screenshot showed one column with each Virtue's score box at the far
right edge of a 700px panel. The step is now 740px (`--bp-sheet-cols`). A 678px Split View window
is still one column.

**U2. Larger iPads in landscape lost the tablet type scale. Fixed.** The one-pixel bump for touch
tablets stopped at 1199px, leaving out iPad Pro 11" (1210) and 13" (1366, 1376). It now runs 740
to 1399px.

**U3. No safe-area handling. Open.** No `env(safe-area-inset-*)` and no `viewport-fit=cover`.
In the installed (Home Screen) app the toast and drawer bottoms can sit under the home indicator.
Not changed, because `viewport-fit=cover` also moves content under the notch in landscape and that
needs a real device to get right.

**U4. Dialogs and the on-screen keyboard. Open, needs a device.** Dialogs are centred and capped
at `84dvh`, which does not shrink for the keyboard.

**U5. Desktop has the smallest text of any device. Open, a design call.** Body is 13px and labels
11px on every non-touch screen, including 1440p and 4K. Sizes are in `px`, so a larger browser
default font has no effect (zoom works).

**U6. Content Admin's record form had no maximum width. Fixed.** At 2560px its inputs ran about
2,000px wide. Capped at 960px.

**U7. The GM's Drama panel stacks four full-width buttons on desktop. Open.** At 1920px each of
Spend, Begin Session, Reset and Record Surrender is about 1,500px wide, using 110px of height for
four short actions. `.action-grid` would put them on one row.

**U8. 320px fails eleven routes. Open.** Horizontal overflow on Home (340px wide), Campaign with
an active encounter and Combat (341), Creating the World (353) and the print sheet (322); two
pairs of overlapping hit areas on the sheet (an `i` against its neighbour). The app bar also wraps
to two rows. 320px is the first-generation iPhone SE and Display Zoom on small iPhones.

**U9. Phone landscape works but is tight.** At 844×390 the app bar and sheet header take about
105px, 27% of the height, and both stay pinned. Passing, noted for a device check.

**U10. Breakpoints have drifted from the stated system. Open.** `layout.css` says 600, 768 and
1024 are the only ones; viewport queries also exist at 560, 720, 820, 840, 1160 and 1800 (and now
740).

**Test matrix.** `harnessConfig.mjs` gains 844×390, 744×1133, 1210×834, 1366×1024 touch and
1366×768. 320 is left out until U8 is fixed, because it would turn CI red.
